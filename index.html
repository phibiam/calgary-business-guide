5 Financial Blind Spots That Could Sink Your Business

1

No key person insurance

What happens to your business if you're suddenly unable to work? Most owners have no plan.

2

Relying on personal savings with no continuity plan

When the business hits a rough patch, personal savings take the hit — silently eroding your family's future.

3

Life insurance not tied to business loans or partnerships

If you've signed a personal guarantee on a business loan, your family may inherit that debt.

4

No group benefits strategy for retaining employees

A well-structured benefits plan can be a stronger retention tool than a pay raise — at a cost that might surprise you.

5

Mixing personal and business financial risk

When your finances are entangled, every business risk is a personal one. There's a cleaner way.
